# Contract Drafting Portfolio

## Overview

This project demonstrates practical understanding of commercial contract drafting, review and clause analysis.

## Contracts Covered

- Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)
- Master Services Agreement (MSA)
- Statement of Work (SOW)
- Vendor Agreement
- Service Agreement
- Employment Agreement
- Licensing Agreement
- SaaS Agreement

## Key Clauses Analysed

### 1. Confidentiality
Purpose, scope of confidential information, exclusions and confidentiality obligations.

### 2. Indemnification
Allocation of third-party and contractual risks between the parties.

### 3. Limitation of Liability
Allocation and limitation of financial exposure arising from contractual breaches.

### 4. Intellectual Property
Ownership and licensing of pre-existing and newly created intellectual property.

### 5. Term and Termination
Contract duration, renewal mechanisms and termination rights.

### 6. Governing Law
Determination of the applicable legal framework.

### 7. Dispute Resolution
Mechanisms for resolving contractual disputes.

### 8. Data Protection
Responsibilities concerning personal and business data.

- Contract negotiation awareness
