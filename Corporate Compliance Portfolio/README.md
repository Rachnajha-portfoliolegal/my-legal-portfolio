# 05. Legal Compliance Risk Matrix

A risk matrix helps the legal team identify and prioritise potential compliance issues.

| Compliance Area | Potential Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Priority | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Statutory Filing | Missed filing | High | Medium | High | Compliance calendar |
| Contract Renewal | Unintended expiry or renewal | Medium | Medium | Medium | Renewal alerts |
| Data Protection | Improper data handling | High | Medium | High | Privacy controls |
| IP Protection | Unauthorised use | High | Medium | High | IP review |
| Employment Compliance | Regulatory breach | High | Medium | High | HR and legal review |
| Corporate Records | Missing documentation | Medium | Low | Medium | Periodic audit |
| Vendor Compliance | Third-party breach | Medium | Medium | Medium | Vendor due diligence |

### Risk Categories

**High Risk**  
Requires timely legal or compliance attention.

**Medium Risk**  
Requires monitoring and documented mitigation.

**Low Risk**  
Routine monitoring may be appropriate, subject to applicable requirements.

### Contractual IP Review

When reviewing a commercial agreement, consider:

1. Who owns pre-existing IP?
2. Who owns newly created IP?
3. What licence is being granted?
4. Is the licence exclusive or non-exclusive?
5. What territory is covered?
6. What happens to IP after termination?
7. Are third-party materials being used?

# 11. Sample Compliance Scenario

### Scenario

A company discovers that a material vendor agreement is approaching expiry and contains a 60-day notice requirement.

### Legal Review

The legal team should:

1. Verify the effective date.
2. Confirm the expiry date.
3. Review the renewal clause.
4. Confirm the notice period.
5. Check whether automatic renewal applies.
6. Review outstanding contractual obligations.
7. Confirm whether the business wants renewal.
8. Determine whether amendments are required.
9. Obtain internal approval.
10. Issue the required notice where applicable.
11. Update the contract management system.

### Potential Risk

Failure to monitor the contractual deadline may result in unintended renewal, service disruption or additional contractual exposure.

### Recommended Control

Maintain a central contract register containing:

- Contract start date
- Contract end date
- Renewal date
- Notice period
- Contract owner
- Key obligations
- Risk level
- Review status
